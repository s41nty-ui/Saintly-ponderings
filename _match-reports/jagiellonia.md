It was cold! I was thinking of excuses to bunk off but was offered a lift to the ground.

We then walked over the bridge into Sunderland to the pub. There was a very heavy police presence and a lot of horse poo. I’m not sure why as we only spotted a few Polish fans.

The crack in the pub was great. Some took early delivery of the superb Mackem in Milan(o) t-shirts. Big up to the design boys Big Andy, Dom & Dave.

Then it was off to the ground, relatively sober.

Sunderland obviously have to rotate their squad a bit and fielded a relatively weakened side. This does give players not regularly in the first team a chance to shine, but not sure any did.

There was a bit of excitement when Polish fans revealed themselves in the North Stand and seemed to be making Nazi salutes. Apparently a few older chaps clipped a few of them and I have seen a video of one toppling down the steps. Police and stewards got them out without much incident and no one seemed too bothered. These foreign fans are very weird,

The first half was laboured and disjointed, but we had the better chances against fairly ordinary opposition. They had a few players who caught the eye, but I didn’t think they were as good as Zagreb.

Angulo missed a really good chance from close range, which he will probably agonise over, and Fofana had a blistering shot that was pushed wide. Apart from that, the two wide men huffed and puffed throughout without really doing anything. Rigg was tidy, but I wanted to see a lot more, and the one time he did charge forward we scored a goal.

That was early in the second half and just as the crowd started to relax, we gifted them a goal from a dodgy Reinildo backpass.

From then on it was turgid stuff and the substitutions didn’t make any difference. 

It’s definitely a pattern. We make a mistake at the back and it impacts the whole team. They seem to lose their mojo.

For me Sadiki and Le Fee really stood out, and Roefs was in good form, as he had to be as the second half ran away from us.

Anyway, more boos at the end which I just don't get. I don’t know who these people are, but they're not the sort of fan we need and I’d rather they stayed away.

Despite not winning I enjoyed the evening, although bloody freezing. Bring on Tottingham.
