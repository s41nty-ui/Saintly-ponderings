Sunderland 0 – 2 Dinamo Zagreb

My brother was up for his first ever European game and we were both very excited. There were a bunch of black clad Zagreb fans on the metro and we had a nice chat. They were not too optimistic and one offered us a deal - Zagreb beat Sunderland, Sunderland beat Chelsea. I was happy with that and impressed he knew our fixtures. Power of the Premier League I suppose. Still not used to being in the top flight.

Our new friends left to join more young men dressed in black, and we headed to the pub. It was great fun in the pub, as usual, then many beers later we were off to the ground.

The bridge to the ground, the lights, the music.. It’s quite something.

In the ground the Zagreb fans were very noisy and didn’t stop singing for all of the game. They seem to have very deep voices. It sounded to me like they were singing the Hokey Cokey most of the time, but I might be wrong.

The noise they made was impressive but it was a bit tedious. I like the atmosphere to be part of the ebb and flow of the game. Scoring seemed incidental. I had the same thoughts in Poznan. Very impressive, but nothing like the roar of approval after a Hume tackle.

As for the game, Zagreb were very bright, had good movement and much better than I was led to believe on the metro. We on the other hand were ponderous, disjointed, error-strewn and lacking imagination.

As has been happening lately, when we concede because of a defensive error, we seem to lose our mojo, and obviously the opposition sit back a bit more and tighten up. Even Xhaka, who when he is bad is still good, was poor (work that one out).

There were a few boos at the final whistle, which is confusing considering where we’ve been, and are actually playing in Europe. My view was it was an off day, and there will be more. Also Regis has to balance his squad and prioritise. Tricky business.

On the metro home we met the same Zagreb fans and I reminded them of our deal.

So having made this deal with a Croatian devil we went home happy in the knowledge that we were going to beat Chelsea the next game.
