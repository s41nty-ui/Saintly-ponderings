Sunderland 1 – 0 Chelsea

My brother had stayed up North for this one and there was something special in the air as we set off for the match.

It might be down to the whiskey in our morning coffee, but we were in a very positive frame of mind and convinced a win was on the cards, after our deal with a Croatian devil.

Quick pint in the local then we jumped on to the bus to go to Sunderland, and sitting on the bus larger than life was big Andy. We just knew it was going to be a good day. Andy’s last match for a while as he is returning to foreign climes, and he was in great form.

In the pub the crack was excellent, and the cheery mood almost belied our dreadful run of results and below par performance.

But Chelsea seem to bring out the best in us!

We had quite a few beers so my rather blurred memory of the match is the whole team played well and those who had been under performing seemed back on top form, for example Reinildo.

Honourable mentions have to go to Ahoka - wow - motm for many; Sadiki - supported Aoka, encouraged him and was everywhere else as well, he is the type of player who is easily taken for granted; Mr Brobby - unplayable; O9 - just when you think this is a level too far, he steps up again. Superb.

It would be quite easy to add every other player to that list. I noticed how well Hume and Meunier played together. Nothing was getting down the right but there were successes all over the field.

There were the usual complaints about the ref. I heard one complain that Mr Brobby was getting nothing from the referee, and this was after we had been given a penalty!

After having watched it 20 times I can conclude it was an obvious penalty.
The Chelsea shout was definitely not a penalty, I watched that 10 times.
Great referee!

Anyway, it was nice to actually talk about football after the game.
